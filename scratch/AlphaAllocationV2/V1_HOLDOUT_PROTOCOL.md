# V1 filter holdout

Compare the current 60-session close-only PatchTST specification with the seven-feature weekly PatchTST. The primary result is the difference in compounded net HRP portfolio return, with daily maximum drawdown reported alongside it.

- Freeze both checkpoints once. Fit using the prior experiment's common eligible stock-weeks from 2021–2024 and select each checkpoint by weekly rank correlation on 2025, with validation MSE as the tie-break. Require all fitting and validation outcomes to finish by December 31, 2025. The baseline takes the last 60 daily close log returns and uses the production five-output MSE, 10-session patches and five-session stride. The challenger uses the prior seven features, 12-Friday context, and weekly return MSE. Both use seed 42, batch size 256, the production 60-epoch cap, and patience eight. This reproduces the baseline architecture and loss in a controlled historical refit.
- Freeze the same August 2026 halal roster and 356-stock cohort used in V2. At each Friday close, score the intersection of rows with both models' inputs available. Report price and news coverage. Verified zero-news windows contribute neutral sentiment. Prior archives and the October audit have opened outcomes through October 2. The primary period starts at the October 5 open, uses Friday decisions October 2–30, and ends at the November 9 open. Both accounts start in cash with USD 10,000. Prior outcomes are used for features and HRP at their decision cutoffs.
- Apply the production 15/20 selector and production HRP math with up to 252 daily returns. Each arm carries its own previous 15 names. Weights and scores use prices available through the Friday decision close. Execute at the next exchange open, allowing fractional shares. Carry drifted holdings and cash, trade the signed dollar differences, and mark NAV each day. Use Yahoo adjusted prices for total-return marks and raw opens for converting traded dollars to shares for fees. Terminal NAV is marked at the November 9 open.
- Model Alpaca retail zero commissions, SEC sell fees at $20.60 per million, and FINRA TAF at $0.000195 per share with a $9.79 cap, rounding sell fees up to cents per leg. Apply the listed-equity CAT fee of $0.000003 per share to all trades and round up once per rebalance. This conservatively applies the higher CAT rate to any OTC trades. Use 10 basis points of combined spread and slippage on each traded dollar as the main case. Report 0, 5, and 25 basis point sensitivities. These fills are a shared simulation of the rebalance, while production uses limits and a sell-wait-buy sequence.
- Publish scores, picks, HRP weights, trading costs, the daily NAV paths, and the paired summary under `data/v1_holdout/`. Treat the five unseen rebalances as a short holdout. The frozen historical roster and revised source prices still limit generalization.
